CC BY-SA 4.0. Full original license and preamble are bundled in
../licenses/SPEKTRAFILM_LICENSE.txt and source license/SPEKTRAFILM_LICENSE.txt.

Pianyu contributors selected the sRGB resource subset from SpektraLab source
d90cf9a005d5c7eb4c0bc1eb9cac17459c2a9289: unused minilab LUT tables and unrelated
CAM16 colour-space tables are omitted. Retained material arrays and LUT values
are unmodified. Mobile UI recipes select existing models and parameters; they
do not constitute new measurements or recalibrated film data. SpektraLab's
own profiles and data are documented in source engine/resources_product/NOTICE.md.

Android Vulkan SPIR-V files are compiled from the included source and overlay;
they are code under GPL-3.0-or-later, not newly measured profile data.
