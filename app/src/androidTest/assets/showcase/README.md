# Public comparison inputs

These seven PNG images (portrait, landscape, night, humanity, phone-landscape, concert, candid) were generated as original digital-style inputs on 2026-11-10. They are synthetic scenes, not camera originals, real people endorsements, or film scans. `ordinary-park.png` and `ordinary-meal.png` are retained as historical test inputs but are not part of the primary showcase. No filter result is supplied by the image generator: `ShowcaseDeviceTest` processes each input through the Android `NativeCore` and `PhotoIO` pipeline, retaining parameters and output hashes.

Prompts requested neutral digital sRGB rendering, natural white balance, no film simulation, no added grain, text, logos, or watermarks. Subjects cover a portrait, landscape, night street, candid everyday scene, phone-sized landscape, concert, and ordinary human moment. Generated inputs may have their own aesthetic biases, so these examples illustrate Android editing behavior and do not establish film accuracy.
